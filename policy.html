Privacy-First Architecture

GhostFit Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

GhostFit ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed from the ground up with a privacy-first, on-device architecture. We believe your fitness journey and body movement data should remain completely private to you.

1. Camera & Video Data

GhostFit uses your device's camera to analyze exercise form and count repetitions in real time.

  • 100% On-Device Processing: All video frames are processed locally inside your device using on-device machine learning models (WebAssembly / MediaPipe).
  • No Recording or Storage: Video feeds are analyzed ephemerally frame-by-frame and immediately discarded. No video or images are ever recorded, saved to disk, or uploaded to any server.
  • No Remote Transmission: No video or biometric identifiers ever leave your device.

2. Microphone & Audio Data

GhostFit provides hands-free voice coaching commands and audio guidance.

  • Voice command recognition is processed locally on your device using speech recognition APIs.
  • No audio recordings are collected, saved, or uploaded to any external server.

3. Workout Data & Local Storage

Your workout routines, rep counts, range-of-motion metrics, and recovery logs are saved solely within your device's local storage (IndexedDB / LocalStorage). We do not require accounts, logins, or cloud databases.

4. Analytics & Third Parties

GhostFit does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. There are zero third-party tracking or advertising SDKs in the application.

5. Security & Peer-to-Peer Linking

If you choose to link a secondary phone camera via the QR code feature, coordinates are transmitted directly between your two devices over local WebRTC peer-to-peer encryption without passing through intermediary relay servers.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our on-device privacy architecture, please contact us via our GitHub repository: github.com/sashwinnnn/Ghostfit.
